## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern, high-trust agricultural exchange designed to bridge grassroots Thai agrarian production with sophisticated wholesale logistics and direct consumer purchasing. The design identity communicates vitality, uncompromised freshness, speed, and structural integrity. 

Rather than leaning into folksy or rustic craft tropes, the visual language embraces Modern Agri-Fintech: crisp data density, lucid real-time inventory metrics, and energetic trade momentum. The interface must inspire mutual confidence—reassuring large-scale institutional buyers of quality and supply predictability, while providing farmers and local brokers with intuitive, legible, and frictionless transaction touchpoints.

Key design principles:
- **Agrarian Vitality Meets Commercial Rigor:** Deep chlorophyll emeralds and lush greens reflect crop vitality, grounded by precise slate typography and warm off-white canvases.
- **Harvest Momentum & Tactical Urgency:** Energetic citrus and amber accents drive real-time lot bidding, inventory countdowns, and quick-liquidation surplus flags without degrading the platform into discount-store chaos.
- **Bilingual Typographic Harmony:** Seamless structural rhythm between Latin numerals/terms and Thai script, preserving balanced baseline alignment, open counters, and immediate legibility across dense data tables and mobile viewports.
- **Uncompromised Operational Transparency:** Explicit verification tiers, lot inspection seals, cold-chain status indicators, and escrow states rendered with zero ambiguity.

## Colors

The palette balances agrarian freshness with the velocity of an active commodities exchange. The base canvas relies on warm stone and cream undertones rather than stark hospital whites, evoking natural sunlight and organic grain.

### Primary Palette (Vitality & Grounding)
- **Primary Base (`#15803D`):** Deep emerald leaf. Deployed for primary brand actions, verified farmer credentials, system confirmations, and secure transaction anchors.
- **Primary Dark / Container (`#166534`):** Forest shadow. Reserved for high-emphasis navigation headers, dark-surface cards, and contrast-critical icons.
- **Primary Bright / Accent (`#22C55E`):** Sprout green. Used for upward market price trends, active online presence, freshly harvested badges, and active toggle fills.

### Secondary Palette (Urgency & Liquidation)
- **Secondary Base (`#EA580C`):** Harvest orange. Deployed for high-velocity bidding triggers, primary conversion actions (e.g., 'Place Bid', 'Buy Lot'), and urgent callouts.

- **Secondary Tint (`#FB923C`):** Soft mandarin. Employed for secondary alert chips, threshold indicators, and hover accents on interactive items.

### Functional Spoilage & Risk Metrics
- **Critical Urgency / Spoilage Warning (< 48 hrs):** Text `#DC2626`, surface `#FEF2F2`, border `#FECACA` (e.g., "ขายด่วนก่อนหมดอายุ" / Urgent Liquidation).

## Typography

The design system employs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for Latin glyphs, accompanied by a clean, geometric looped/modern-loopless hybrid Thai fallback system (such as Sukhumvit Set or Prompt) to guarantee maximum visual cohesion. Plus Jakarta Sans offers broad x-height, generous counter shapes, and friendly yet disciplined curvature, matching the structural needs of Thai vowels and tone marks.

Typographic guidelines:
- **Baseline and Line Height Compensation:** Thai scripts demand adequate vertical clearance for upper tone marks (ไม้เอก, ไม้โท) and lower vowel signs (สระอุ, สระอู). Never compress body line-height below 1.5x on bilingual blocks to prevent diacritic clipping.
- **Tabular Figures & Numeral Alignment:** All bidding logs, price per kilogram values, tonnages, and countdown timers must leverage tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain visual alignment across high-frequency price updates.
- **Micro-Copy Hierarchy:** Bilingual terminology pairs English technical metrics with evocative Thai phrases. The primary language takes the dominant weight (SemiBold/Bold), while the secondary translation sits at Regular weight or a one-step smaller scale (e.g., **Durian Monthong / ทุเรียนหมอนทอง**).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture employs a structured fluid-responsive grid engineered for both high-density commodity displays and mobile field use by growers and aggregators.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `2rem` (32px) margins, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Maximum content canvas constrained to `1320px` to maintain focal density for expansive inventory tables and map views.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `1.5rem` (24px) margins, `1rem` (16px) gutters. Left-rail filters compress into off-canvas or horizontal sliding trays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `1rem` (16px) canvas margins, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters. Market price boards shift from multi-column tables into modular high-cardinality listing cards with anchored bottom interaction bars.

### Density & Cadence Rules
- **Component Padding Scale:** 
  - Micro-badges and status tags: `space-xs` (4px) vertical, `space-sm` (8px) horizontal.
  - Form inputs and standard CTA buttons: `space-sm` (8px) to `space-md` (16px) vertical, `space-md` (16px) to `space-lg` (24px) horizontal.
  - Product & Lot Cards: `space-md` (16px) internal cell padding on mobile, expanding to `space-lg` (24px) on desktop viewports.
- **Surplus & Auction Ticker:** A persistent 40px sub-header bar uses `space-sm` margins to anchor real-time agricultural quotes across provinces (e.g., Chiang Mai Longan, Surat Thani Palm).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is built through clean tonal stacking, razor-thin structured borders, and warm ambient diffusion rather than heavy dropshadows. This keeps the data-dense platform lightweight and readable under harsh sunlight in field conditions.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Backdrop):** `#FAFAF9` (Stone-50). The uninterrupted underlying page canvas.
- **Tier 1 (Base Container):** `#FFFFFF` (White) with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` (Slate-200). Used for auction cards, supplier directory items, and listing grids.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Action & Modals):** `#FFFFFF` combined with a soft, sun-warmed ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Used for bid entry drawers, checkout drawers, and floating action triggers.
- **Tier 3 (Active Attention / Live Countdown):** Highlighted card surfaces utilize a 1px perimeter border tinted with the status hue (`#F97316` for urgent lots or `#15803D` for locked transactions) coupled with an inner ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.15), 0 12px 20px -4px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.08)`.

### Glass & Blur Elements
Sticky table column headers and the mobile navigation dock utilize high-clarity backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over an 85% alpha fill of `#FFFFFF` or `#FAFAF9`, keeping content context visible as users scroll long inventory manifests.

## Shapes

The interface balances welcoming accessibility with the discipline of an enterprise exchange. Roundedness level `2` sets the primary geometry:

- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Select Drawers:** `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px to 1rem / 16px depending on component scale), providing an approachable, tactile click target.
- **Produce & Lot Cards:** `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px) to `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) on desktop containers, offering soft framing for high-resolution farm, harvest, and lot imagery.
- **Status Badges, Urgency Pills, and Metric Chips:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`) to immediately differentiate informative tags from rectangular data cells and interactive cards.
- **Data Table Cells & Group Headers:** Square inner edges flanked by `rounded-xl` perimeter boundary borders to preserve crisp scanning lines for numbers and metrics.

- **Structure:** Crisp white background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `rounded-2xl` clipping boundary.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom 20px controls with a 2px `#CBD5E1` border in inactive state. When selected: `#15803D` fill with a crisp white check/dot. Radio buttons feature an inner concentric white ring.

### Data Tables (Wholesale Bidding & Market Price Index)
- **Header:** `#F8FAFC` background, 12px uppercase typography in `#64748B`, height 40px, pinned during vertical scrolling.
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle hover states (`#F1F5F9`), height 56px, vertical dividers absent, horizontal divider 1px solid `#F1F5F9`.
- **Price Trend Cells:** Integrated micro-sparklines displaying 7-day price fluctuations alongside dynamic badges (`+3.4%`, `-1.2%`).
- **Tabular Figures:** Numbers aligned right; item descriptions aligned left.
